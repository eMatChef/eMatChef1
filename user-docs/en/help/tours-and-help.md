# Tours and help

They have different jobs.

## Tours (in the app)

Tours walk through the **current UI**: clicks, dialogs, checklist. They stay in the app (`Help → Tours`) and are not part of this public site.

## This help (public)

Here are the **workflows in writing**: no login, no department data. Use them to look things up, share a link, or let an assistant read the page next to a screenshot.

## In the app

- **Help** button (bottom right): matching public page
- Menu **Help → Tours**: start a guided tour
- Menu **Help → Documentation**: overview with links here
